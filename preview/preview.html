📌 How to Use This Preview

Desktop: Hover over menu items to see dropdown menus

Mobile: Resize your browser to less than 1024px width, or open on mobile. Click the hamburger menu (☰) to open the navigation drawer.

Note: This is a static demo. On your live site, the categories and brands will be populated automatically from your WooCommerce products.

✨ What You're Seeing

This is exactly how the ADVapes navigation bar will look when installed on your WooCommerce site with the Razzi theme.

🎯 Desktop Experience

Clean horizontal menu with hover dropdowns. All menu items fit on a single line without wrapping.

📱 Mobile Optimized

Hamburger menu opens a right-side drawer. Touch-friendly with collapsible sections.

🔄 Dynamic Content

Categories and brands automatically populate from your WooCommerce products. No manual updates needed!

⚡ Performance

30-second cache with auto-refresh. Fast loading with minimal database queries.

📁 Files Needed for Installation

To install this on your WooCommerce site, you need these three files:

  • advapes-nav.php - Navigation logic and functions
  • advapes-nav.css - Navigation styling (same as this preview)
  • header.php - Modified Razzi header template
